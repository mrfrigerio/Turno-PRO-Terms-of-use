Privacy Policy

Overview
Turno Pro is not designed to track you for advertising or to sell personal data. To operate accounts, subscriptions, and notifications, we process identifiers and contact details strictly as described below.

Data we do not use for those purposes
We do not sell personal data. We do not use third‑party analytics frameworks to track in‑app activity for targeted ads.

Account, name, and email
When you create an account or sign in with a provider, we may process: your account identifier, email address, and name (or equivalent supplied by the provider or by you). Purposes include authentication, syncing preferences and data tied to your account, support, and compliance with this policy and the Terms. The account and cloud infrastructure used by the App follows the practices of the contracted provider.

Subscriptions and app stores
Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple and/or Google. The App uses a subscription management service to tie subscription status to your in‑app account, including recognizing the same subscription on iOS and Android when you use the same account, subject to the stores’ and provider’s rules. For that purpose, your in‑app account identifier and, where available, email and display name may be sent to the subscription provider, which processes them under its own privacy policy. We do not store payment card data on our systems; payment is handled by the stores.

Push notifications
If you allow notifications, we use a push notification provider that may associate message delivery with your in‑app account identifier and, where applicable, email or name for subscriber profile management, as described in that vendor’s documentation. The operating system may use technical device identifiers for delivery. You can revoke permission in device settings.

Support
If you email support@turnoproapp.com, we will use the message content and address only to respond to your request.

Retention and security
We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above and as required by law or by involved vendors. Reasonable measures are used to protect the information we process.

Children
The App is not intended for unsupervised use by children under 13; use by minors should be supervised by a parent or guardian.

Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the new version where allowed by law.
